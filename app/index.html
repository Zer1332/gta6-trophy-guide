    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay, but free roam continues after the credits and almost everything can be finished then. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they disable trophies and saving. The only trophy reported as possibly missable is Positive Mental Attitude: do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, before that chapter ends.

      How this guide is built

      • Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles (55 trophies, 28 hidden until earned).
      • Every guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites its sources beside it — GTABase and PowerPyx first, with other launch-era guides where they add something. Where no source has confirmed the exact steps yet, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
      • Missable flags and the roadmap stages are built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order.
      • Works fully offline: this page loads only its own files (style.css, app.js, data/trophies.json) and makes no network request. Progress lives in your browser’s local storage; export writes a file you can import back later.
      • Unofficial fan guide. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games. No Rockstar logo, artwork, or game imagery is used.
